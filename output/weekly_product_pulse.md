# Weekly Product Pulse — 2026-11-25

**Scope:** 30 public reviews (2026-09-01 to 2026-11-10).

## Top Themes
1. **Exit load / Redemption charges confusion** — 11 mentions (36.7%), avg rating 2.5/5
2. **UI / Navigation & report access** — 7 mentions (23.3%), avg rating 3.3/5
3. **Expense ratio / TER transparency** — 6 mentions (20.0%), avg rating 3.8/5

## What Users Are Saying
- “The exit load FAQ says 1% within 1 year but I redeemed on day 364. Still charged.” — review R013, 2026-10-01 (rating 3/5)
- “I sold my HDFC Large Cap units and 1% exit load was charged. This was not clear at purchase.” — review R007, 2026-09-15 (rating 2/5)
- “Exit load deducted silently. Nobody told me about the 1% charge. Very bad experience.” — review R003, 2026-09-05 (rating 2/5)

## Key Observation
Exit load (1% if redeemed within 1 year) is the single most recurring confusion (12 mentions). Users describe the charge as unexpected or unclear at the time of redemption, especially near the 1-year boundary. This is a communication gap, not a pricing dispute.

## Action Ideas for Product Team
1. Add a pre-redemption confirmation showing applicable exit load before the user confirms.
2. Surface an exit-load explainer card on the scheme page and in the FAQ with the 1-year rule in plain language.
3. Add a support macro with the standardized fee explanation for agents handling redemption queries.
